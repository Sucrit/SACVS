## 2024-03-15 - Accessible Modals, Drawers, and Dropdown Menus
**Learning:** In this codebase, the custom UI components for modals (`Modal.tsx`), drawers (`Drawer.tsx`), and flyout menus (`ActionMenu.tsx`) were missing fundamental ARIA roles and labels (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby` for dialogs, and `role="menu"` with `aria-haspopup="menu"` for flyouts). Icon-only close buttons were also consistently missing `aria-label`s, which causes screen readers to read them simply as "button".
**Action:** When building or modifying custom overlay components (modals, drawers, tooltips, flyouts) in this repo, ensure `role` and related ARIA accessibility attributes are included, and always ensure `aria-label` is present on icon-only interactive elements.
